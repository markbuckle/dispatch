## Carousel - 1080 × 1350 (4:5), PDF

4:5 is the correct ratio - it takes the most feed height. Export as PDF, not images.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 - Cover | One claim, `display-m`, max 8 words. Mark top-left. Slide count bottom-right. |
| 2 - Stakes | Why it matters, one sentence + one number |
| 3–7 - Body | One idea each. A heading at 28px and max 3 lines of 18px body. Or one screenshot. Or one code block at 16px mono. |
| 8 - Recap | 3 lines, the three things |
| 9 - CTA | One line + `dispatch.com`. No "follow me for more". |

Max 9 slides. 64px padding. Never more than 40 words on a slide.

## Post copy

- **First line is everything.** LinkedIn truncates at ~140 characters on mobile. Lead with the number or the claim.
- No "🧵" no "1/" no "Thread:".
- No hook-then-withhold ("Here's what I learned… 👇").
- Line breaks between short paragraphs - a wall of text doesn't get read.
- 3 hashtags maximum, at the end, lowercase: `#email #deliverability #devtools`.
- One link, in the post body. LinkedIn deprioritises external links, so put the substance in the post and the link as a footnote.
- No engagement bait. No "agree?", no "thoughts?", no poll where a statement would do.

## Good openers

- "We checked 40,000 marketing emails against Outlook 2016. 71% had at least one property that silently fails."
- "A password reset that doesn't arrive doesn't throw an error. It becomes a support ticket three days later."
- "Your email provider telling you it accepted the request is not the same as the email arriving."

## Bad openers

- "Excited to share…"
- "I've been thinking a lot about email lately 🧵"
- "Unpopular opinion:"
- "Most people get this wrong."

## Never

Emoji in the graphic · a headshot in a product post · "Swipe →" arrows on every slide · gradient backgrounds · stock photography · a testimonial as a graphic · humble-brag framing · tagging people who aren't in the post
