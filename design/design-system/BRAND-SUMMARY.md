# Dispatch - brand summary

## What it is

An email API for developers. Transactional and marketing email, sent over a clean HTTP interface, with the delivery observability that competing products treat as an afterthought.

## Why it exists

Email infrastructure is the least interesting part of shipping a product and the most likely to fail silently. Dispatch makes the failure visible: which message, which client, which DNS record, which line of HTML.

## The name

To dispatch is to send something off promptly and with purpose - an order, a message, a package put in motion. Not "deliver" (passive, arrival-focused). Not "send" (generic). Dispatch carries intent and speed.

## Personality

Precise. Unhurried. Legible. Candid. Well-made.

## Archetype

**The Craftsman.** Builds tools other builders trust. Shows its work. Doesn't oversell, doesn't apologise.

## Anti-archetype

**The Hype Machine.** Gradient hero, 3D render, "supercharge your email", a funnel where the docs should be.

## The promise

You will know exactly what happened to every email you send.

## Tagline

**Email that arrives.**

Alts:

- Email infrastructure you can debug.

## Voice in a paragraph

Dispatch talks like a good colleague explaining a system: second person, short sentences, the specific noun rather than the abstract one. It says "your DKIM record is missing" rather than "a configuration issue was detected". It never sells inside the product, and outside the product it sells by being precise - a number, a client name, a status code. When there is nothing specific to say, it says less.

## What someone should feel in five seconds
